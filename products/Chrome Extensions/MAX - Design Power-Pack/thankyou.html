Getting Started in 3 Easy Steps

1

Pin the Extension

Click the jigsaw puzzle piece icon in the top-right corner of Chrome, locate MAX - All for Designers, and click the pin icon.

2

Open Popup or Side Panel

Click the pinned MAX icon. You can run MAX as a Popup Overlay or dock it as a Side Panel next to your page.

3

Start Designing

Navigate to any webpage, open MAX, and begin collecting assets, taking screenshots, or picking colors instantly!
